## Example questions

- _Find a sea surface temperature dataset and plot its data for any one time step_
- _What was the mean SST in the Black Sea on June 01, 2024?_
- _Find the location of the max SST value in the Black Sea on June 01, 2024._
- _Draw a bar chart comparing the max SST's in Chesapeake Bay, Delaware Bay, and San Pablo Bay in the month of June 2024._
- _Plot the SST maps for Jan 1, 2023 and Aug 1, 2023 stacked vertically._
- _Show the daily average SST within the Chesapeake Bay over the the months Jan 2020 and Jan 2024 as two separate time series on the same plot._
- _Show the daily average SST within the Chesapeake Bay over the year 2024 as a time series._
- _Visualize the SST raster within 10 miles of the coast of Florida on Oct 01, 2024._


## Debug mode

Debug mode can be enabled by typing in `!debug`.

In debug mode, your messages will be directly executed as code in the agent's execution environment.

To exit debug mode, enter `!chat`.
